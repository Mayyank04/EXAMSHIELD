# ExamShield Hackathon & Jury Demonstration Guide

This guide outlines the recommended 5-minute and 10-minute presentation flows for showcasing ExamShield to hackathon judges, national examination authorities, and cybersecurity evaluators.

## 5-Minute Quick Pitch Walkthrough

### 1. Landing & 3D Security Core (30 seconds)
- Open `http://localhost:3000/`.
- Show the editorial aesthetic, headline, live defense metrics, and the interactive **Three.js 3D Encrypted Core**.
- Click **"ENTER EXAMSHIELD"** to enter the Command Center.

### 2. Command Center & Real-Time KPIs (45 seconds)
- Highlight the **System Security Score (98/100)**, Protected Papers, Active Armored Transports, and the live chronological security feed.
- Switch the active user role using the top-right RBAC switcher (e.g. from `SUPER_ADMIN` to `SECURITY_OFFICER`).

### 3. Cryptographic Tamper Station (60 seconds)
- Navigate to **"Protected Papers"** (`papers`).
- Select `PAP-001` (Physics NEET-UG 2027 Set A).
- In the **Tamper & Test Lab**, modify 1 character in the text box and click **"Re-Compute SHA-256 Hash"**.
- Point out how the hash diverged completely and the system flagged an integrity violation.

### 4. Armored Transport Radar & GPS Breach (60 seconds)
- Navigate to **"Armored Radar"** (`transport`).
- View the vehicle marker along the authorized corridor.
- Click **"Simulate Physical Box Compromise"**.
- Watch the box switch to `TAMPER_LOCKED`, light jump to `520 Lux`, and an emergency alert pop up.

### 5. Two-Party Handover Protection (45 seconds)
- Navigate to **"Two-Party Handover"** (`handover`).
- Demonstrate that attempting to transfer the compromised box is **BLOCKED** by the zero-trust consensus policy.

### 6. AI Leak Detection & Immutable Blockchain (60 seconds)
- Navigate to **"Leak Detection"** (`leak`) and click **"🚨 Leak Sample"** -> **"Execute AI Semantic Leak Analysis"**. Show the **92% match** against protected questions.
- Navigate to **"Immutable Ledger"** (`blockchain`) and click **"Verify Ledger Integrity"** to demonstrate verifiable cryptographic proof.
